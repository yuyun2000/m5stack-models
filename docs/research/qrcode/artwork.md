# U173 body artwork

Three exact-product body labels are directly rasterized from the supplied original production contours: front QR-CODE mark, rear SCAN MODULE/UART/I2C diagram, and small side UART/I2C SWITCH label. The corresponding supplied preview and exact U173 official front/back/side photos establish the same variant. Packaging labels and unrelated circular artwork are excluded.

Only final ordinary PNGs are distributed. The original glyph outlines and colours remain, with conversion winding and gradient opacity corrected to match the supplied preview. This is original-artwork rasterization, with no photographic crop, enlarged preview pixels or replacement typography. Source vector files, private table, private source URLs, filenames, mappings and conversion scripts remain private.

- Front: 2400 × 1704 pixels, 21.134 × 15 mm, centered `[0,14.5,8]`.
- Rear: 2228 × 2949 pixels, 19.652 × 26.009 mm, centered `[0,7.6,-8]`.
- Side switch: 1327 × 850 pixels, 4.682 × 3 mm, centered `[-12,4.5,-3.45]`.

All three use shallow surface projection onto existing native shell faces; cutouts and source geometry stay intact. Registration is photo-derived. PNG decoding, nonempty registered triangles and physical invariance with `artwork:false` pass; browser pixel re-review is deferred.
