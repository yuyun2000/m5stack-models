# U086 AMeter artwork

The two final PNGs are **reference-redraw** assets. They are newly drawn text, shapes and fixed diagram strokes from exact U086 official front/back photographs, rasterized at 1920×3168 and 1920×4080. No product photo is cropped, enlarged or used as a runtime texture.

The front label retains plus/minus groups, ±4A, pink A circle, hazard stripe, Meter and ISOLATION. The rear retains its own current diagram, fuse 4A, 0.05R, ADS1115/0x48, CA-IS3020S, EEPROM/0x51 and PORT.A I2C labels. No VMeter voltage-divider diagram, 0x49/0x53 print, QR pattern or unreadable identifier is invented. This is fixed printed appearance; it provides no electrical or measurement behavior.

Typography, line arrangement, colours and surface registration are visual reconstructions. Actual moulded surfaces, PCB, connector cavity and indicator lens exist without artwork; `artwork:false` preserves their complete geometry and transforms. Only final ordinary PNGs are distributed; source documents, private mapping, original vectors, reference photos, code generators and intermediates stay outside the repository. Product names, original printed designs and trademarks retain their respective rights. See `artwork.json` for public sources and asset hashes.
