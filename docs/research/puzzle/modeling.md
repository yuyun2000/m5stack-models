Snapshot: 2026-10-26. Stage: `modeled`; full browser/reference acceptance remains deferred, and this product is not `verified`.

The exact U193 structure retains the assembled rear shell and actual 8 × 8 isolation grid, components 1 and 2, all **4,538 native triangles**, at scale 1 under one common translation. The own official documentation, schematic, dimensions and product photographs confirm 64 WS2812E-1313 RGB packages, J2 input Grove, J1 output Grove, populated decoupling and the central M2 fixing. There is no MCU, reset switch, button, display, sensor, motor or joint. The reconstructed physical assembly includes the PCB, its real central drilling, 64 individual 1.3 mm package bodies, 256 metal package terminals, 64 independent emissive encapsulations, an actual thin translucent front diffuser, populated backside capacitors and fixing shank, two rear-open Grove housings, latch/guide details, eight real metal contacts and installed leads. The two large rear mounting bores remain genuine empty shell bores, but the complete product correctly shows the real dark PCB behind them, as in the exact-model rear photograph. They are not falsely claimed to pass through the entire LED matrix. The native grid has 64 genuinely hollow cells; the physical diffuser closes their front independently of artwork.

The model axes are +X right, +Y top, +Z front. `pixel-0` through `pixel-63` are physical row-major positions, starting at front-view top left and ending bottom right, at 2.8 mm pitch. Centres are `x=(column-3.5)*2.8`, `y=(3.5-row)*2.8`; indices are appearance layout, not automatic schematic D numbering or a WS2812 protocol/address implementation. Each is an ordinary externally driven `Led`, starts off, and changes only its own real package lens material. Billboard glows are suppressed to preserve the physical grid isolation. No automatic animation or brightness/current limiter runs.

`matrix` accepts an atomic external appearance command:

```js
puzzle.handle({type: 'matrix', target: 'matrix', colors: Array(64).fill('#204060'), brightness: 0.3});
puzzle.handle({type: 'matrix', target: 'matrix', rgba: new Uint8Array(256), brightness: 1});
puzzle.handle({type: 'led', target: 'pixel-17', color: '#ff4010', brightness: 0.5});
```

A frame must supply exactly one of `colors` or `rgba`. `colors` has exactly 64 RGB integer, hex RGB string, named color or finite normalized `THREE.Color` entries. `rgba` has exactly 256 integer bytes, with each alpha multiplying that pixel's normalized visual brightness. `brightness` is finite 0–1 and defaults to 1. Every frame entry is validated before any pixel changes; rejected length, invalid color, non-finite value or out-of-range byte preserves all previous packages. `getState()` returns `{width:8,height:8,order:'row-major-top-left',pixels:[{color:'#rrggbb',brightness:0..1},…]}` by reading the actual 64 Led states each time. Direct individual Led commands therefore immediately appear in the matrix snapshot. Neither pixel nor matrix updates emit input.

`grove` is the real J2 input at the bottom, and `out` is J1 output at the top. Both are genuine `Port` objects with four separate contacts, pin hover grid, a rear-open housing and exact-model printed row handedness. Their mating frames use +Y outward and +Z toward the Unit rear, reflecting the actual socket orientation; this deliberate half-turn allows the common cable's pin row and latch to match both the physical contacts and the rear labels. Pin 4 is labelled IO2 as physically printed. `GroveModule` supplies the common four-wire cable and `unitFrame` pose API. A second `GroveModule` can attach to `out`, yielding paths such as `unit/out/matrix`; this establishes geometry and ownership only. Upstream colors, IO signals, power, voltage and WS2812 data never propagate automatically.

`Bridge` is an independently exported `Device` for the single supplied rigid joining accessory. It retains its complete native carrier and rear retaining cap, and adds the actual small PCB, central drilling, two black four-chamber contact strips and recessed spring contacts. It has no fabricated mechanical or electrical Port, no command component and no implicit attachment behavior. The original CAD places its centre at `[0,-12,-3.75]` in the Unit frame; this is retained as source placement evidence, not asserted as a validated fit or production assembly datum.
