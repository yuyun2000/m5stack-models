# TimerPWR artwork

Four high-resolution PNGs are reference redraws of exact U189 official photographs: front title and button guide from 11.webp, rear diagram from 12.webp, and USB/battery labels from 9.webp. They contain typeset text and drawn shapes; no product photograph crop or private source file is redistributed. `artwork.json` records public reference URLs, hashes, registration and raster dimensions.

The front labels leave the physical OLED aperture open. Its pixels come only from the external `lcd` framebuffer. The back label is an adhesive film that spans the original rear inspection recess, matching the product photo; PCB and panel are real solids behind it. `artwork:false` disables all four labels without changing physical geometry, indices or matrices.

Typeface and optical spacing remain approximations. No unreadable serial, QR, hidden rating or firmware screen has been invented. Marks and print retain their respective rights.
