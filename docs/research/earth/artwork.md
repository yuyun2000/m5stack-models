# Earth sticker provenance

Both final PNGs are `source-vector-raster`: the supplied U019 artwork contains complete standard English EARTH and Chinese 土壤湿度 glyph paths for the same 16 × 12 mm rear sticker. The default follows the standard retail appearance in the exact official U019 product photo. `stickerVariant: 'education-cn'` opts into the Chinese education appearance in the supplied preview.

The converter preserved path geometry but returned every path with `fill:none`, including both overlaid title variants. Fill colors and the selected visible title were restored from the matching references before rasterization at 2400 × 1800. This paint/layer recovery is explicitly documented; the final PNGs do not claim a byte-identical export of the production artwork. No product-photo pixels were cropped or used to conceal geometry.

Only the ordinary final PNGs and their public registration code are distributed. Source documents, private links, table records, vectors, converters and intermediate images remain excluded. Original printed designs, product names and marks retain their respective rights. The source map records final PNG hashes, sizes and the official public reference; it does not disclose the private source mapping.
