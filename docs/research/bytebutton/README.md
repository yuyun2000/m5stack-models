# Unit ByteButton — U192

Official U192 has eight momentary tactile keys, nine WS2812C-2020 RGB LEDs and two parallel PORT.A Grove connectors. The listing gives 88 × 24 × 12 mm; the official drawing separately dimensions the caps' 2 mm projection. This portrait model therefore exposes housing nominal **24 × 88 × 12 mm**, and complete cap envelope **24 × 88 × 14 mm**. +X is right, +Y is toward CH7, +Z is the operating face.

Native CAD components 0/3 are the complete assembled front/rear shells; 4/7 are the original eight-way and end lightpipes. One rigid coordinate transform applies to all four; displaced duplicate parts are omitted. Native shell mounting bores, seams, operating openings and dual connector cutouts stay intact. The eight original RGB exit surfaces are assigned separate LED materials without changing or duplicating their triangles. Solid black caps, concealed actuator stems, actual tactile packages and metal covers close the eight native button apertures. PCB scallops, retaining bores and connector relief preserve intentional openings. Installed electronics, reconstructed PCB, cap/stem details, 0.2 mm cap stroke and Grove interiors are appearance inferences, not manufacturing geometry or mechanical tolerances.

`press-1` … `press-8` map to the molded **CH0 … CH7**, in that order. The CAD's raised digits fix CH0 at lower Y and CH7 at upper Y. Each uses the existing momentary `Button`: `press(source)`, `release(source)` or `cancel(source)` move its real cap and stem; cancel returns to rest without emitting a click. External RGB controls are `rgb-1` … `rgb-8` beside CH0 … CH7 and `rgb-9` for the end indicator. These IDs identify physical location; they do not assert U1–U9 serial chain order. Lights remain independently controlled through `Led.set({color,brightness})`, with no automatic response to keys.

`Model` and `GroveModule` share those paths. `grove` is the bottom mating input used by the standard four-wire cable wrapper; `grove-out` is the upper cascade socket and remains available for downstream attachment. For a module attached at `unit`, resolve `unit/press-1`, `unit/rgb-1` and `unit/grove-out` through its host. `sensor` is only a geometry anchor. This package provides shape, external visual state and input intent; I2C, registers, debounce, electrical and firmware behavior belong to another runtime.

`sources.json` records immutable CAD source/hash, native component selection, transform, official dimensions, reference image hashes and inferred details. Preserve `licenses/M5_Hardware-MIT.txt`. Both fixed-shell labels are now included as reference-redrawn PNGs. Private sticker CSVs, vector originals, mappings, generators and photo crops remain absent.

`smoke.json` records finite geometry/envelope checks, 20 clear mounting rays, PCB screw/lightpipe bore FrontSide wall tests, all eight independent press/release/cancel paths, independent RGB control, both physical Grove openings, nested paths and attachment/detachment/resource release. It is a necessary construction/API smoke, with no browser or hardware verification. Stage remains `modeled`.


Printed front channel/title and rear pinout labels are included as `reference-redraw` PNGs. `artwork:false` disables them without changing physical geometry, controls or connectors. Sources, placement and bounded checks: [artwork.md](artwork.md), [sticker-smoke.json](sticker-smoke.json).
