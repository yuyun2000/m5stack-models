# U170 LoRaE220-920 artwork

Three ordinary high-resolution PNGs are enabled by default and explicitly marked `reference-redraw`: front LoRa920/certification mark, rear LoRaE220-JP920MHz/model/mode/UART diagram, and the fixed S1ON1/2 marking. Exact-model references, SHA256 and physical registrations are in [artwork.json](./artwork.json). The two main labels are1944×4368px; the small switch legend is624×744px.

All text, paths and colour blocks are newly code-drawn and rasterized directly. No product photograph pixels are cropped, projected, sampled or enlarged. The native shell window and the real switch slots remain holes in physical geometry; printed layers use only existing outward shell/deck triangles. White sliders are independent actual meshes, not painted representations. `artwork:false` changes no physical attributes, indices or world transforms.

The exact rear print's M0/M1 text is preserved as static artwork. Its relationship to the current official ON/OFF table and footnote is under official feedback00ea6ae3dfc5419192d9e28306a0bdbe; no firmware mode semantics are implemented. Font metrics, colours, small diagram spacing and physical registration are reconstructed appearance details. Only necessary PNG decode/local geometry checks were performed; browser/exhaustive visual acceptance is deferred.

Only final ordinary PNGs are distributed. Private source tables, source artwork, vectors, raw references, generators and intermediates are excluded. Product names, printed designs and trademarks retain their respective rights.
